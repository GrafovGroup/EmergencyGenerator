# EmergencyGenerator.ca — Codex Master Prompt v1.0

## Project directive
Create a premium, bilingual (English and Canadian French), Canada-wide commercial and industrial emergency generator and backup power website. Position EmergencyGenerator.ca as a direct solutions provider coordinating equipment, qualified installation, rental, repair, testing and maintenance services. Do not position it as a directory, referral broker, or consumer-only shop. Do not claim inventory, certifications, emergency coverage, manufacturer authorization, or in-house technical capabilities without verification.

## Business scope
Commercial and industrial customers only. Serve factories, warehouses, logistics, property management, office towers, healthcare, senior living, data centers, telecommunications, municipal facilities, water/wastewater, food processing, agriculture, construction, mining, energy and institutions. No homeowner/residential sales positioning.

### Core service groups
1. Generator sales and procurement: diesel, natural gas, propane, bi-fuel, standby, prime, mobile and custom industrial units; new/replacement/upgrade.
2. Generator rentals: short-term, long-term, trailer-mounted, construction, planned shutdown, outage response, temporary distribution, cables and fuel tanks.
3. Engineering coordination, sizing, installation and commissioning: electrical design by qualified professionals, site assessments, pads/enclosures, exhaust, fuel systems, ATS integration, permits and commissioning.
4. Preventive maintenance: inspections, scheduled servicing, fluid and filter changes, battery checks, maintenance plans, records and compliance support.
5. Diagnostics and repair: breakdowns, troubleshooting, parts, major repairs, alternators, controls, ATS faults; emergency response only where operationally supported.
6. Testing: load-bank testing, exercise tests, commissioning tests, fuel quality testing and reporting; describe CSA C282 applicability carefully and avoid blanket certification claims.
7. Power infrastructure: automatic transfer switches, switchgear, UPS, battery energy storage, hybrid systems, remote monitoring, controls, paralleling and synchronization.
8. Fuel and support: diesel delivery coordination, fuel polishing, storage tank services, winterization, spill-prevention coordination, decommissioning and recycling.
9. Power continuity planning: risk assessment, backup-power readiness, lifecycle replacement and outage contingency planning.
10. Parts and accessories: informative catalogue and request-for-quote flow at launch; ecommerce optional pending owner decision. Do not imply live inventory or checkout.

## Lead generation and forms
Primary CTAs: REQUEST A QUOTE, RENT A GENERATOR, REQUEST SERVICE, PLAN AN INSTALLATION. Forms should collect company, contact, city/province, facility type, service needed, preferred timeline, generator size in kW/kVA if known, fuel type, purchase/rental, runtime, installation location and optional notes. Emergency request captures urgency and callback number; do not promise immediate dispatch. Protect against spam with honeypot, server-side validation, rate limiting and appropriate CAPTCHA if needed. Formspree endpoint TBD; never invent one. Show centered accessible success message and clear completed fields after confirmed success. Protect data and avoid logging sensitive form contents.

## Copy and credibility
Write concise Canadian B2B copy, clear service descriptions, realistic capabilities, buyer-focused benefits and accessible CTAs. Present business as direct accountable provider with qualified trade partners where needed, not a lead marketplace. Never claim 24/7 staffing, Canada-wide physical locations, certifications, equipment ownership, OEM dealership status, guaranteed response times or customer testimonials without confirmation. Include legal safety notes and defer electrical/fuel work to licensed professionals.
